NEFAKT YouTube

NEFAKT YouTube is an internal application used by the NEFAKT content team to manage YouTube Shorts publishing workflows and review channel performance using Google and YouTube APIs.

The integration connects authorized YouTube accounts to an n8n-based automation system. It supports content publishing, retrieval of channel and video analytics, and editorial reporting.

How access works

The application requests Google OAuth authorization from the account owner. Access is limited to the permissions granted during authorization and may be revoked at any time in the owner's Google Account settings.
