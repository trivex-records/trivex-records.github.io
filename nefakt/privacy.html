Privacy Policy

NEFAKT YouTube · Effective November 17, 2026

This policy describes how NEFAKT YouTube, an internal application operated for the NEFAKT project by Trivex Records, uses information accessed through Google OAuth and YouTube APIs.

Information accessed

When an authorized account owner grants access, the application may access the YouTube channel identity, video metadata, publishing capabilities, and channel or video performance statistics, according to the scopes approved in the Google authorization screen.

Purpose of use

The information is used to automate authorized video publishing, analyze YouTube Shorts performance, prepare editorial reports, and improve content planning for the NEFAKT channel. The application is not offered as a public consumer service.

Storage and security

The integration uses an n8n-based workflow system. OAuth credentials and operational data may be stored within the application's configured infrastructure. Access is restricted to authorized administrators. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

The application does not sell Google user data. Data may be processed by Google and YouTube APIs as necessary to provide the described functionality, and by infrastructure components needed to operate the application.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed for authorized operational, analytical, or security purposes. The account owner may request deletion of data associated with the integration by contacting the project operator through the developer link below.

Revoking access

Google account owners can revoke the application's access at Google Account — Third-party access. Revoking access prevents further authorized API requests but does not automatically delete previously stored operational records.

Contact

For privacy inquiries and deletion requests, contact the project operator via the Trivex Records GitHub profile.
